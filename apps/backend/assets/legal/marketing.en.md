# Consent to Marketing Messages

If you agree, {{controller.name}} may send you e-mails and app notifications about Casalume news, new styles, campaigns
and discounts. For this your name, e-mail address, push token and app preferences (e.g. styles you like) are processed.

- Consent is optional; refusing does not affect your use of the Service.
- You can withdraw it free of charge at any time in **Profile → Privacy** or with the link in every message.
- Commercial electronic messages are sent under Turkish Law No. 6563 and its regulation; when required your consent is
  registered with the Message Management System (İYS).

See the Privacy Notice for details. The Turkish version prevails.
