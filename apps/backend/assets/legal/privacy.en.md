# Privacy Notice

This notice explains, under Article 10 of the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 ("KVKK"), how your personal
data is processed when you use the Casalume app and website. The Turkish version prevails.

## 2. What we process

- **Identity and contact:** your name (if you give it), e-mail address, and the identifier and e-mail Apple or Google send
  us when you sign in with them.
- **Images:** room, building or garden photos you upload and the designs created from them. Location and device data
  (EXIF) is removed when a photo reaches our servers. Please avoid photos that show people.
- **Customer transactions:** design history, tools and styles used, credit movements, subscription status, messages to
  the design assistant.
- **Security:** IP address, a one-way hash for your device (the device id itself is not stored), session records, push
  token, security and error logs.
- **Marketing:** only with your explicit consent, your preferences for news and offers.

## 3. Purposes and legal grounds

| Purpose | Legal ground (KVKK Art. 5/2) |
|---|---|
| Creating your account, generating designs, managing subscriptions and credits | Performance of a contract (c) |
| Processing your photos with AI providers to create designs | Performance of a contract (c); Art. 9 for transfers abroad (below) |
| Account security, preventing abuse and fraud, error tracking | Legitimate interest (f) |
| Statutory retention, notices and requests from authorities | Legal obligation (ç) |
| News and offers | Explicit consent (Art. 5/1) |

## 4. Who we share data with

Only as far as needed to provide the service:

- **AI providers** (e.g. Google Gemini, fal.ai, Anthropic): photos, design instructions and chat messages, only to create
  designs and suggestions.
- **Hosting and storage** (servers and object storage).
- **Payments and subscriptions:** Apple App Store, Google Play and the subscription platform (RevenueCat). We never see
  card details.
- **Notifications and e-mail:** Expo push service and an e-mail delivery service.
- **Error tracking:** error reports, which carry no personal data.
- **Authorities:** only when the law requires it.

Some of these providers are located abroad. Transfers abroad rely on the safeguards in KVKK Art. 9 (standard contracts)
or on the explicit consent you give in the AI processing notice.

## 5. How we collect it

Electronically: from what you enter in the app and website, from Apple/Google when you sign in with them, from the app
stores, and automatically while you use the service (server logs).

## 6. Retention

- Uploads not used in a design are deleted after 7 days.
- Designs are kept until you delete them or your account.
- Deleting your account deletes the data linked to it; with Sign in with Apple the permission at Apple is revoked too.
- Records we must keep by law are kept for the statutory period.
- KVKK application records are kept for 2 years after closing, as proof of how they were handled.

## 7. Your rights (KVKK Art. 11)

You may ask the data controller to:

- learn whether your data is processed and request information about it,
- learn the purpose of processing and whether it is used accordingly,
- know the third parties in Turkey or abroad it is transferred to,
- have incomplete or incorrect data corrected,
- have it deleted or destroyed under KVKK Art. 7,
- have corrections and deletions notified to those third parties,
- object to a result against you that arises solely from automated analysis,
- claim compensation for damage caused by unlawful processing.

## 8. How to apply

- In the app: **Profile → Privacy → KVKK request** (verified by your account),
- from your registered e-mail address to {{controller.email}},
- by KEP to {{controller.kep}},
- or in writing to {{controller.address}}.

Your application is answered free of charge within **30 days** at the latest. If the action involves an extra cost, the fee
set by the Personal Data Protection Board may be charged. You can also download all your data with **Download my data**
in the app.
