# Consent to AI Processing and Transfer Abroad

To create designs, the photos you upload, your design instructions and your messages to the design assistant are sent to
AI service providers (e.g. Google Gemini, fal.ai, Anthropic). Their servers may be located outside Turkey.

- The data is used only to create the design, region mask, quality check and suggestions you ask for.
- Location and device data (EXIF) is removed from photos before they are sent; your name and e-mail are not sent.

By accepting, you give your **explicit consent** to this processing and to the transfer to providers abroad under KVKK
Art. 9. Consent is optional and you can withdraw it at any time in **Profile → Privacy**. Without it AI design generation
is unavailable; your account and saved designs are not affected.
